## Detailed description
Turn your Edge new tab into a private cinema.

Video New Tab replaces the default new tab page with a looping video background and a real-time rendered liquid-glass search bar. Frost, lens refraction and chromatic dispersion are all adjustable.

Features
• Video background: built-in demo video, plus local video files (drag & drop) or direct video URLs.
• Playlist: manage multiple videos, previous / next, shuffle and auto-rotation (1-60 minutes).
• Liquid-glass search bar: WebGL real-time frost, edge lens refraction and dispersion; 5 sliders: frost, tint, refraction, lens thickness, dispersion.
• Page elements: show / hide clock, date and search bar; Bing, Baidu and Google search providers.
• Playback: a glass menu in the top-right corner switches videos and toggles sound; volume, playback speed and fit mode are adjustable.
• Local only: your videos stay in the local Edge extension storage and are never uploaded. The extension itself collects no data.

How to use
1. After installation, press Ctrl+T to open a new tab.
2. Click the "≡" menu in the top-right corner -> Settings to change the background video and tune the glass effect.
3. You can also drag a local video file into the page or paste a direct video URL.

Notes
• MP4 (H.264) or WebM recommended.
• The liquid-glass effect requires WebGL; a CSS glass fallback is used when unavailable.
• Settings and videos are stored on your device only; they are removed when you uninstall the extension.
